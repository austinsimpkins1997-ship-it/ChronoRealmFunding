What this evidence supports

The recorded v1.5 Windows run completed all 11 declared major gates. The inherited MASTER bounded suite also completed 14/14 recorded steps. The public package preserves those outcomes while removing machine/user identifiers and private meeting material.

What it does not claim

It is not independent certification, universal correctness, clinical validation, or proof that every deployment will behave the same way. Open formal and independent-host questions remain explicitly open.

Claim boundary

  • PASS is local software/evidence-integrity evidence for this environment only.
  • The human-welfare gate is architectural policy, not medical diagnosis or clinical treatment.
  • Health conditions, grief, survivors, disabilities, and LGBTQIA+ identity are not forbidden states.
  • The policy blocks intentional inducement/exploitation of preventable harm.
  • v1.4 materials integration remains synthetic/software validation and does not reproduce the cited hydroxyapatite experiment.
  • Independent certification is not established.
  • Independent second physical host and Step17 -> Step17B formal soundness remain open unless separately evidenced.
  • Negative results and unresolved findings remain preserved.

Evidence principle

A failed proof, incomplete test, counterexample, resource exhaustion, or negative result remains evidence. Later revisions should narrow, supersede, or correct prior claims without silently rewriting what actually occurred.
